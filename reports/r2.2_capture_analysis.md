### C9. Final recommendation

**(ii) Capture is sufficient but regime-narrow.** A sweep against this data will give a calm-regime-conditional answer; defaults chosen here must not be promoted live without a second capture in a higher-vol regime.

**Justification (≤ 3 sentences):** 143 fully-observed markets clear the size threshold and books are uniformly healthy (zero I01-empty rejects, sub-200 ms book staleness, dense CLOB feed) — the capture mechanics are sound. But median realised σ is 0.247 with 93 % of markets in the < 0.50 calm bucket, so any flag that interacts with vol regime (`SIGMA_HAIRCUT`, the per-zone walked-edge floors, the `force_close_*` and SL-decay knobs) will be tuned against the calm tail. Additionally, F07's 144-of-144 watchdog firings tell us that any sweep parameter implicitly assuming a non-trivial `fair_enh` is currently tuning against a flat-pricing reality — that's a separate rabbit hole the sweep cannot fix.

**Can validate (data is informative):**
- `WALKED_VWAP_EDGE_MIN` and per-zone `EDGE_MIN` overrides for `early` / `mid` / `sweet_spot` (63 PASS + 58 REJECT samples; non-trivial walked-edge distribution from −0.85 to +0.74).

- `WALKED_VWAP_SIZE_ON_WALKED_EDGE` against the 63 entries that passed the gate at varying walked-edge thinness.
- Book-quality assumptions baked into the gate (`MIN_TOP_OF_BOOK_SHARES_RATIO`, `WALKED_VWAP_PARTIAL_OK`).

**Cannot validate (data is missing or trivial):**
- `WALKED_VWAP_EDGE_MIN_LATE_GAMMA` and `EDGE_MIN_OVERRIDE_LATE_GAMMA` — 0 entries in the late_gamma zone.
- High-vol-regime parameter behaviour — only 7 markets at σ ≥ 0.50, 1 at σ > 0.80.
- Anything assuming `fair_enh` differs from `fair_base` — F07 watchdog says they're equal in 100 % of markets.
- `SIGMA_HAIRCUT` directional bet on calm vs elevated regimes — sample is calm-dominated; the haircut's effect would only be measurable on the 13 % elevated tail.

**Recommended second capture before promoting any default to "on":** target a 12-hour window with median realised σ ≥ 0.50 (e.g., a session aligned with a known-volatile macro event or BTC technical breakout), and ensure `late_gamma` participation by holding the daemon through enough markets that at least 5 hit the late_gamma zone.
