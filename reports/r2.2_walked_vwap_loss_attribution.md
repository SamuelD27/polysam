## (a) Fill-realism cost — the irreducible tax

Phase 4's dual-PnL design records both `pnl` (real, against the walked-VWAP rewritten entry) and `pnl_mid` (counterfactual, against the parent's pre-walk mid quote) on every closed walked trade. Both use the same exit price (`WALKED_VWAP_EXIT_ENABLE=0` default, so exits are mid-quoted in both).

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Total `pnl` (walked, real) | **−$12.41** |
| Total `pnl_mid` (counterfactual, mid-quoted entries) | **+$51.41** |
| **Fill-realism gap (`pnl_mid − pnl`)** | **+$63.82** |
| Per-trade gap: mean / median / min / max | $1.01 / $0.08 / $0.00 / $34.10 |
| Trades with `pnl_mid > pnl` (walked priced honestly worse) | 56 / 63 |
| Trades with `pnl_mid < pnl` (walked priced better than mid — should be 0) | 0 / 63 ✓ |
| Trades with `pnl_mid == pnl` (full loss; entry difference cancelled) | 7 / 63 |

**Reading.** The fill-realism gap ALONE is $63.82 — larger than the absolute loss. If walked had used the parent's mid-quoted entries the strategy would have netted +$51.41 (close to enhanced's $203.96 / refined's $259.71 trajectory, scaled down because walked also rejected 48 % of candidate entries). The signal works; the price the bot pays at the CLOB just eats it.

Mean gap is $1.01 / trade, but the distribution is heavy-tailed — a single trade had $34.10 gap. That outlier is a high-walked-edge entry (see (b)) where size scaled up and the entry-vs-mid spread was wide.

**Critical constraint.** The fill-realism gap is real money the bot pays at the CLOB. It is NOT something flag tuning can wave away. Any flag change that produces a "win" by reducing this gap is doing so by either (i) rejecting more trades (reducing exposure) or (ii) sizing down (reducing the per-share spread × shares). The gap per share is set by Polymarket book physics, not by our flags.

**Q: Do high-walked-edge entries SL at a lower rate than low?**

**Q: Within the SL bucket, is the loss driven by (i) entry over-priced post-fee, or (ii) SL threshold firing too late?**

Mostly (i), amplified by position sizing. Evidence:
- Mean fill-gap rises with walked_edge: $0.20 → $0.66 → **$5.37**. The high-edge bucket pays 27× the per-trade fill-realism cost of the low bucket.
- Mean hold time on high-edge SL exits is 58 s vs 22–28 s elsewhere — the SL threshold isn't firing fast; if anything it's holding longer because `adaptive_sl = clamp(0.5·edge + 0.05, 0.05, 0.20)` saturates at 0.20 for edge ≥ 0.30, giving high-edge entries the WIDEST SL band.
- High-edge entries also have larger absolute size (the parent's `compute_position_size` saturates at MAX_RISK for edge ≥ 0.25, so high-walked-edge entries are at full size on top of the wider SL).

**The high bucket's losses are a position-sizing × SL-band amplification problem, not a threshold-too-late problem.** The 7 high-edge SL exits average **−$5.91** each — that's the bulk of the entire session loss.

## Implications for the flag sweep

Stated as questions, not answers — the sweep's job is to test these.

1. **`MIN_TOP_OF_BOOK_SHARES_RATIO` (default 1.0).** All 15 `top_smaller_than_request` rejects were 100 %-win-rate trades for refined. Sweep this knob downward: 0.5, 0.25, 0.0. Risk: relaxing it accepts partial-fill exposure.
2. **`WALKED_VWAP_PARTIAL_OK` (default 0).** Same family. 15 rejects could become 15 partial-fill entries instead of misses.
3. **Position-sizing on high walked-edge.** The high (≥ 0.248) bucket is the loss concentration: 7 / 29 SL exits eat $41.39 of the $59.34 total SL loss. `WALKED_VWAP_SIZE_ON_WALKED_EDGE` (default 0) recomputes size from walked_edge. Sweep direction has to be analysed: with the flag ON, size SHRINKS for thin books (mid_edge > walked_edge), which would help. But for the high-walked-edge bucket where mid_edge ≈ walked_edge, the flag does nothing. A more direct intervention may be a CEILING on the linear interpolation, not a re-derivation.
4. **`SL_ABSOLUTE_AGAINST` (default unset).** A hard cap on SL drawdown. With high-edge entries averaging −$5.91 per SL exit on $3.69 average size, an absolute SL of e.g. 0.10–0.15 would have capped these losses. Sweep this knob meaningfully — its effect is concentrated in the high bucket.
5. **`SL_DECAY_ENABLE` (default 0).** Mean SL hold-time is 58 s on high-edge SLs. Decay would tighten the SL band as time runs down, potentially firing earlier. Worth sweeping but second-order to (4).
6. **`WALKED_VWAP_EDGE_MIN` raise.** The data argues AGAINST raising the floor — low-edge bucket is net positive ($+7.10). Sweeping this knob up will likely make things worse.
7. **Per-zone overrides (`EDGE_MIN_OVERRIDE_*`, `WALKED_VWAP_EDGE_MIN_*`).** `late_gamma` had 0 entries — sweep over these zones is data-empty. `mid` (41 entries), `early` (13), `sweet_spot` (9) — only `mid` has enough sample for tuning.

The sweep's priority order based on this attribution:
1. (4) `SL_ABSOLUTE_AGAINST` — direct attack on the high-edge SL loss concentration.
2. (1)+(2) `MIN_TOP_OF_BOOK_SHARES_RATIO` and `WALKED_VWAP_PARTIAL_OK` — recover the $140 of `top_smaller_than_request` opportunity cost.
3. (3) Position-sizing knobs — secondary; the linear-interp saturation may need more than `SIZE_ON_WALKED_EDGE` can address.
4. (5) `SL_DECAY_ENABLE` — third-order, useful for hygiene.
5. NOT recommended for sweep on this data: raising `WALKED_VWAP_EDGE_MIN` or per-zone equivalents.

The sweep CAN address the gate-over-rejection cost ($114). The sweep CANNOT address the fill-realism tax ($63.82) — that's a Polymarket book physics constraint and would only shrink as a side effect of taking fewer / smaller trades.

## Caveats on this attribution

- **Calm-regime data** per the capture report. Median realised σ = 0.247 (BTC historical "normal" 0.5–0.7). High-edge entries in elevated-vol regimes may behave differently — there's a tail of cases where the mid quote was honest and the book walked into a reverting move. Don't promote any sweep-derived default to "on" without a higher-vol capture confirming the high-edge SL concentration persists.
- **n=63 entries split into 15 / 33 / 15 buckets.** The high and low buckets each have 15 trades — not enough to make any single-trade exclusion shift the picture. If a single $34 fill-gap outlier (max in (a)) is in the high bucket, the high bucket's −$21.52 net partly reflects that one trade.
- **Counterfactual at mid pricing is upper-bound.** The "what if walked took these" estimate uses refined's mid-quoted PnL minus an average $1.01/trade fill tax. The actual walked-vwap pricing on those 58 rejects is unknown — could be higher per-trade for `top_smaller_than_request` rejects specifically (where the book was thin enough to reject), so the +$114 recovery estimate is optimistic. A floor of +$50 is defensible; a ceiling of +$150 is not.
- **Fill-realism tax is computed against a counterfactual that doesn't pay fees.** `pnl_mid` uses `entry_price_mid` which is the parent's pre-walk mid quote — fee-free by construction. Refined / enhanced's actual recorded PnL also uses this same fee-free mid-quoted entry path. So the $63.82 gap is the SUM of (book-walk slippage above mid) + (bell-curve taker fee on the buy side). The fees portion is the operator's actual cost; the book-walk portion is also real.
